Manage your team

As the owner, you can add managers to your store directly from your dashboard. Managers can log in with their own email, upload products, change prices, and add flyers.

Your control stays absolute. You choose exactly what they can see and do. You can remove a manager instantly if they leave the business.

Run specials and flyers

Attract more customers by adding flyers and running weekly specials. When you add a product, mark it as "On Special".

Set the special price and exactly how long the special runs for. Our system automatically displays this to customers.
