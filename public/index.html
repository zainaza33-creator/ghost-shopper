How Ghost Shopper Works

Enter your delivery address to check for delivery availability and browse the partner stores available in your area.
2
Add what you need
Choose your products, add them to your cart and check your order before paying.
3
Pay online
Pay securely when you place your order. Once your payment is confirmed, the order is sent through for processing.
4
We collect and deliver
A local rider collects your order from the store and brings it straight to your door. You can follow the progress of your order from your account.
About Ghost Shopper

Your local stores,
delivered to you.

Ghost Shopper makes it easier to shop from local stores and have your order delivered to you.

Instead of travelling to the store yourself, you can browse participating shops online, choose your items and have a local rider collect and deliver them.

We're starting in Verulam, KZN, working with local businesses and riders in the area. As more stores join, you'll have more places to shop from.
