About Ghost Shopper

Connecting our community with the stores they love, delivered to their door.

Meet the Founder

Mohammed Zain Saragee

Founder & CEO

Ghost Shopper was born from my belief that everyday shopping could be made simpler while creating more opportunities for local businesses.

As an entrepreneur, I have always been interested in identifying gaps in the market and finding practical ways to solve real-world problems. I saw an opportunity to bring local stores together on one online platform.

My vision for Ghost Shopper goes beyond delivery. I want to help local retailers reach more customers, compete in an increasingly digital world and grow their businesses without having to build their own platforms from scratch.

Starting locally in Verulam, KwaZulu-Natal

Because I believe meaningful growth begins with serving your own community well.

“
01

Our Mission

Ghost Shopper was built to make everyday shopping easier for the people of Verulam and the surrounding areas. We believe you shouldn't have to spend hours driving to the shops, finding parking, and carrying heavy bags just to get your daily essentials.

By partnering with local stores, supermarkets, butcheries, and pharmacies, we allow you to shop online and have a trusted local rider deliver your order straight to your door.

“
02

Supporting Local

We are proud to be a local platform. When you order through Ghost Shopper, you are supporting businesses right here in your community. We don't mark up the prices of the products you love.

You pay the store's price, plus a simple delivery fee.

“

My Vision for Ghost Shopper

My long-term ambition is to grow Ghost Shopper into a leading online shopping and local delivery platform across South Africa. I want to connect communities with the businesses around them, create earning opportunities for local riders and make convenient shopping accessible to more people.

I believe technology should not only make life easier for customers; it should also open doors for small and independent businesses.

Ghost Shopper is more than a delivery service. It is my vision for bringing local shopping into the digital age, one community at a time.
