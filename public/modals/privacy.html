Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Ghost Shopper respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ghost Shopper may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices and rights available to you.

Ghost Shopper operates under the trading name CloudSkunk. Ghost Shopper is the customer-facing brand through which the delivery service is provided.

References to "Ghost Shopper", "we", "us" or "our" in this Privacy Policy refer to the operator of the Ghost Shopper service trading under the name CloudSkunk.

By using Ghost Shopper, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Ghost Shopper, we may collect the following information.

2.1 Account and Contact Information

This may include:

  • name;
  • mobile phone number;
  • email address;
  • account information; and
  • information you provide when contacting Ghost Shopper.

2.2 Delivery Information

To deliver an order, we may collect and process:

  • delivery address;
  • location or address information provided during checkout;
  • delivery instructions;
  • contact details needed to complete the delivery; and
  • information relating to delivery attempts.

Your delivery information may need to be provided to the relevant delivery driver and participating store where necessary to fulfil your order.

2.3 Order Information

We may collect information relating to your orders, including:

  • products ordered;
  • participating store;
  • quantities;
  • prices;
  • delivery fees;
  • order date and time;
  • order status;
  • payment status;
  • delivery information;
  • refunds;
  • wallet credits; and
  • order-related communications.

2.4 Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

Ghost Shopper may receive information relating to the payment transaction, such as payment status, transaction references, amount and other information necessary to identify and reconcile a payment.

Where payment details are processed directly by a payment provider, Ghost Shopper does not intend to store complete payment card details.

Payments made through Ghost Shopper may appear on your bank, card or payment statement under the name CloudSkunk or another applicable payment-processing descriptor rather than "Ghost Shopper".

2.5 Device and Technical Information

When you use Ghost Shopper, certain technical information may be collected automatically by Ghost Shopper or its technology and service providers.

This may include:

  • IP address;
  • browser type;
  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • technical logs;
  • connection information; and
  • information about how the service is accessed or used.

This information may be used to maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, prevent abuse and improve the service.

2.6 Location Information

Ghost Shopper may process location information where necessary to provide delivery-related services or where you choose to enable location services on your device.

You can control location permissions through your device or browser settings.

The delivery address you provide during an order may still be required even if you do not enable device location services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your personal information to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • process orders;
  • communicate with participating stores;
  • arrange deliveries;
  • provide delivery information to drivers;
  • process and reconcile payments;
  • provide wallet credits;
  • process refund requests;
  • communicate with you about your orders;
  • respond to customer support requests;
  • investigate complaints and order issues;
  • prevent fraud and misuse;
  • maintain the security of Ghost Shopper;
  • maintain and improve the service;
  • troubleshoot technical problems;
  • comply with legal or regulatory requirements; and
  • communicate important service-related information.

Where you have provided the necessary consent, we may also use your contact information to send promotional messages, offers or other marketing communications.

4. Order and Delivery Information Sharing

Ghost Shopper may need to share relevant information with participating stores and delivery drivers in order to fulfil an order.

For example, a delivery driver may need access to:

  • the customer's name;
  • delivery address;
  • contact information;
  • delivery instructions; and
  • information necessary to identify and deliver the order.

Participating stores may receive information necessary to prepare and fulfil the customer's order.

We aim to provide only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service to be performed.

5. Service Providers and Third Parties

Ghost Shopper may use third-party service providers to operate and maintain parts of the platform.

These may include providers involved in:

  • payment processing;
  • hosting and infrastructure;
  • authentication;
  • database services;
  • mapping and location services;
  • communications;
  • notifications;
  • security; and
  • technical support.

These providers may process information on behalf of Ghost Shopper as necessary to provide their services.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Ghost Shopper may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to:
