Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Ghost Shopper uses cookies to improve your experience, analyse site traffic, and personalise content.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly (e.g., cart, login).
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use our site.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to show relevant ads (only with your consent).

How We Use Cookies

  • Keep you logged in and maintain your cart.
  • Remember your delivery address and preferences.
  • Analyse site traffic and improve performance.
  • Show relevant promotions and special offers.

Your Choices

  • Accept All: All cookies will be enabled.
  • Decline: Only essential cookies will be used.
  • Manage: You can change your preferences anytime.

Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services (Google Analytics, Facebook) that may set their own cookies. These services have their own privacy policies.

Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them.
