1. Information We Collect

When you register as a driver, we collect your name, surname, email address, and mobile number. As you complete your profile, we also collect:

  • Vehicle registration and driver's license details.
  • Copies of your ID and driver's license (photo uploads).
  • Your banking details (for payment processing).
  • Your live location and online/offline status while the driver app is active.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Verify your identity and eligibility to be a driver.
  • Dispatch delivery orders to you based on your location.
  • Process your earnings and payouts to your bank account.
  • Communicate with you regarding order updates, administrative changes, and support.

3. Location Tracking

Important: When you toggle yourself as "Online" on the dashboard, Ghost Shopper tracks your location. This is used to assign you orders, map your route to the customer, and allow customers to track their delivery progress. When you go "Offline", location tracking stops.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share necessary information with:

  • Customers: Your first name, vehicle type, and location while you are on an active delivery.
  • Payment Processors: To facilitate your earnings payouts.
  • Law Enforcement: If required by South African law.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security (via Firebase and Google Cloud) to protect your data. Your ID photos and banking details are stored securely and are only accessible to authorised Ghost Shopper administrators.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you, or request that your account and personal data be deleted (subject to legal retention requirements). To do this, please contact the admin team.
